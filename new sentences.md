1. I have a gut feeling [that] + clause
    - a gut feeling 直觉
    > I have a gut feeling he's lying.
    > I have a gut feeling things will get better.
    > I have a gut feeling that something is wrong.
2. be better off 会更好 / be well[badly] off 日子过的好/不好
    > You are better off without me.
    > You are better off going along.
    > You are better off sleeping early.
    > I am better off staying at home.
    > We are better off saving money.
3. couldn't be bothered to + verb  懒得
    > I couldn't be bothered to go out.
    > I couldn't be bothered to cook today.
    > I couldn't be bothered to wait in the long queue.
    > I couldn't be bothered to talk to you right now.
